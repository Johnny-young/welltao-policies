1. Data We Collect

1.1 Account information: when you sign in with an email address and password, we store your email address; when you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address the provider shares with us (Apple may provide a private relay address).

1.2 Profile: the display name, gender, and age you provide in the app; used to compute the constitution score and tailor wellness guidance.

1.3 Assessment answers (per-question responses): uploaded when you submit the assessment, used to generate your wellness guidance and stored with your account.

1.4 Assessment outcome (converted scores and judgments per constitution): uploaded and stored with your account.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or device fingerprint.

2. Sensitive Health Data (Explicit Consent)

Gender, age, assessment answers, and assessment outcomes are considered sensitive health-related personal data. We process them only after you grant explicit consent, solely to compute the constitution assessment and generate wellness guidance. You may refuse or withdraw this consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect other features, and you may delete the related data.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to provide the constitution assessment and AI-generated wellness guidance service. Wellness guidance is generated by an AI model (DeepSeek) on our server; the AI does not train on your data and does not retain it beyond the request. We share data with carefully selected processors solely for the following purposes:

  • Supabase (Auth, Postgres, Edge Functions) — account authentication, database storage, and server-side processing. Hosted in the United States under GDPR-compliant data processing agreements.
  • DeepSeek (AI) — encrypted transmission of assessment answers and outcomes only, used solely to generate your personalized wellness guidance on demand.
  • RevenueCat — handles in-app purchase receipts and entitlement state; we do not see your payment card details.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — processes payments for one-time unlock of personalized reports. We do not see or store your payment card number or payment account.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics providers.

4. Storage & Security

Your sign-in session is managed by Supabase Auth and stored only on your device. The session is refreshed automatically while you remain signed in and is revoked on sign-out, account deletion, or after extended inactivity. Draft answers that you have not submitted remain on your device and are never uploaded. Submitted assessment answers and outcomes are stored on Supabase infrastructure with HTTPS encryption in transit and access controls at rest. We retain your personal data until you delete your account; account deletion permanently removes all of your data as described in section 5.

5. Your Rights

Regardless of where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Export your assessment history and wellness guidance ("data portability").
  • Restrict or object to specific processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

EU/UK residents may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. California residents may exercise the rights above and request information about data sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising (we do none).

You can delete your data under Profile → Delete Account, and request an export under Profile → Export My Data (a copy is emailed to your registered address within 7 days). For any other request, contact us at support@welltao.top — we respond within 15 business days at no charge.

6. Children

Welltao is intended for adults and is not directed to children. You must be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age required in your country or region) to use the app; if you are under that age, use the app only with the involvement and consent of a parent or guardian.

7. International Transfers

Where data is transferred outside your country of residence, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent safeguards approved under GDPR.

8. Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be presented to you in-app and require your renewed consent before they take effect. Continued use after the effective date of any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or complaints: support@welltao.top — we respond within 15 business days.
